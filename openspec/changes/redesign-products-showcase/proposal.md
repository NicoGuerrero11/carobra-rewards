## Why

Productos places repeated account status and explanatory banners before commercial discovery. Customers need a clearer view of the three solutions, their purpose, and their relationship with Rewards.

## What Changes

- Put a concise introduction and three compact product cards in a desktop row first; keep customer products in a compact secondary section.
- Use prominent official logos in proportionate brand headers, aspirational copy and goal-specific contact labels for saving, car protection and retirement planning. The page generates interest; the advisor handles commercial details.
- End with a compact human invitation to contact the Carobra team. Preserve existing email destinations while the team decides its preferred contact channel; do not invent an assigned advisor.
- Remove the earlier explanatory dialogs, advisor preparation checklists and generic Rewards/explore-benefits promotion.
- Remove unsupported point/level promises. Display only the owner-confirmed amounts in compact labels above contact: Skandia 600, Quálitas 150, Modalidad 40 600. These are presentation values, not activation of reward issuance or confirmation of crediting conditions. Use brand assets for local visual review, pending publication approval.
- Preserve customer authentication, real product states and existing contact behavior; no database or Rewards changes.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `products-showcase`: Focused product discovery, branded presentation and accessible detail exploration.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing customer-safe commercial-discovery and semantic-color requirements remain in force.

## Impact

Frontend Productos page, local product presentation data/assets, component styles and browser tests. No APIs, dependencies or production data changes.
