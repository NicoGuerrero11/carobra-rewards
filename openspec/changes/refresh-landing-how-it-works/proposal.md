## Why

The public how-it-works section retains an oversized left heading, redundant quote and large stacked cards. A compact three-step sequence will match the recently refreshed landing sections and explain the process clearly.

## What Changes

- Use a centered “Empieza en tres pasos” heading on a white background.
- Replace the split layout and card timeline with three concise steps connected by a subtle line, horizontal on desktop and vertical on mobile.
- Use blue numbered circles and the existing Carobra typography, palette and compact type scale.
- Remove the quote and avoid adding another call to action. Keep `#experiencia` and both existing navigation links to it.
- Preserve the sequence of registration, product validation and account-specific availability without changing the actual workflows.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `landing-how-it-works`: Accessible and responsive public three-step explanation of joining Rewards.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Frontend landing composition, a scoped static component and browser tests. No API, authorization, database, dependency, commit or deployment changes. Existing uncommitted work remains intact.
