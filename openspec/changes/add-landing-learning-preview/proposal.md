## Why

The public landing now previews coupon brands but does not communicate the approved learning offer. Add a compact, truthful introduction to Cursos and Bienestar that invites registration without exposing protected learning content.

## What Changes

- Add an independent learning section immediately below the coupon roll.
- Present separate Cursos and Bienestar tabs with a curated image-card carousel, three visible cards on desktop and touch scrolling on mobile.
- Reuse the translucent side-arrow treatment, compact typography and CAROBRA colors.
- Show real titles and minimum levels from approved catalog manifests; wellbeing is available from Bronze, courses retain their assigned levels.
- Keep the introduction short, show conditional access copy and a registration CTA; do not embed players or publish the full catalog.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `rewards-public-discovery`: Add a compact, accessible public learning preview with accurate space and level labels.

## Impact

Frontend landing component, curated public preview metadata, tests and local asset presentation. Existing customer access, playback, course progress, Bonda APIs and database remain unchanged. No new runtime dependency, deployment or commit.
