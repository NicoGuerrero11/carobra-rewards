## Why

The public product cards are text-heavy and the catalog is buried inside Productos. The user wants compact, logo-first cards and an independent catalog section, leaving course presentation for a later design discussion.

## What Changes

- Replace the large carousel cards with a compact responsive grid emphasizing product marks and one short description per product.
- Reuse existing Skandia and Quálitas assets and the existing typographic Modalidad 40 identifier. Preserve Infinity with a plain text identifier until its approved logo is provided; do not fabricate artwork.
- Preserve the existing registration destination and all four product families without promising eligibility, pricing or automatic contracting.
- Move the prospective catalog into its own labeled section and anchor, keeping its upcoming status and text-only brand names.
- Do not add courses or change authenticated products, benefits, eligibility, or backend behavior.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `rewards-public-discovery`: compact logo-first product discovery and a standalone prospective catalog section.

## Impact

Public landing markup/styles, a small product presentation component and browser tests. Existing local assets reused; no new dependencies, API calls, database changes or external publication.
