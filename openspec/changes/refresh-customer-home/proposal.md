## Why

Inicio still describes legacy assignments and future gift cards instead of the live benefits, courses, wellness and product offer. Customers need a compact, useful home that leads to current experiences and remains usable when an individual service fails.

## What Changes

- Replace oversized account panels with a concise greeting, real level/balance and approved next-level requirements only.
- Show up to four eligible coupon cards using the catalog's imagery, logos and visual treatment.
- Show recent unfinished learning with chapter-aware continuation, or eligible course discovery; feature a wellness video and article separately.
- Add a compact product offer with existing brand assets, approved display points and unchanged product/contact destinations.
- Retain a small recent-activity summary, remove generic no-action prompts and future gift-card advertising from Inicio only.
- Isolate catalog failures, bound parallel reads and preserve authentication and server eligibility. No writes or partner crawling on home navigation.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `customer-home-discovery`: Compact, eligible discovery and learning continuation on Inicio.

### Modified Capabilities
- `rewards-customer-site-experience`: Home prioritization and truthful state sourced from the current authenticated module APIs, with partial failure isolation.

## Impact

Frontend Inicio, shared coupon presentation, authenticated course-list progress metadata and tests. No dependencies, migrations, customer/progress writes, reward rules, production deployment or changes to the public landing.
