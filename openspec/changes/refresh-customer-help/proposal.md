## Why

Ayuda only offers two state-dependent answers and repeated support email links. It does not explain the current benefits, learning, progress, or product experience and its legacy visual hierarchy no longer matches the customer site.

## What Changes

- Replace the oversized help layout with compact Carobra typography, five topic shortcuts, accessible FAQ accordions, and useful internal destinations.
- Provide concise, source-grounded answers for account/levels, benefits, courses/wellness, points/activity, and products; include common troubleshooting guidance.
- Keep general answers visible when the authenticated account projection is unavailable while retaining server-owned contextual guidance when available.
- Separate Rewards support from product information. Display `soporte@carobra.com` explicitly as a non-operational example, without a mailto link or invented service hours.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `rewards-customer-action-center`: Categorized resilient help and truthful contact guidance for current customer features.

## Impact

Frontend Ayuda route, scoped presentation/content, isolated browser tests, and OpenSpec artifacts only. No production writes, migrations, changes to business rules, external messages, new dependencies, commits, or deployment.
